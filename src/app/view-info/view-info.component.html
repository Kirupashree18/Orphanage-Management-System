Adoption Information

📌 Eligibility Criteria for Adoption:

  • Must be at least 21 years old
  • Must have a stable income
  • Must pass the background verification
  • Must complete the adoption training program

📌 Required Documents:

  • Government-issued ID (Aadhar, Passport, etc.)
  • Proof of Income (Salary Slips, Tax Documents)
  • Residence Proof
  • Medical Clearance Certificate
